THE SHORTCODE

Three ready-made variants. Only data-ref is required for referral tracking — everything else is optional.

Basic embeddark theme · USD → NGN default 
<!-- CrossTrade swap widget -->
<script src="https://crosstrade.app/ct-widget.js"
        data-ref="YOUR-CODE"></script>
Custom pair + amountlight theme · ZAR → USDC · 5,000 preset 
<script src="https://crosstrade.app/ct-widget.js"
        data-ref="YOUR-CODE"
        data-theme="light"
        data-from="ZAR" data-to="USDC"
        data-amount="5000"></script>
CMS / div variantfor page builders that strip script tags 
<div class="ct-swap-widget" data-ref="YOUR-CODE" data-theme="dark"></div>
<script src="https://crosstrade.app/ct-widget.js"></script>

THREE LEVELS OF REFERRAL INCOME

Every widget embed carries a referral code. Codes can be chained — when someone you referred starts embedding, you earn on their traffic too, three levels deep.

LEVEL 1
30%

Your embeds

You place the widget with your code. Every swap through it pays you 30% of the 0.8% CrossTrade fee.

LEVEL 2
20%

Partners you referred

Someone registers with your code as their referrer. Every swap through their embeds pays you 20% of the fee.

LEVEL 3
10%

Their partners

One more level down: swaps through your partners' partners' embeds still pay you 10% of the fee.
