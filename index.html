Why Stellar

Built for
moving value

Most chains bolt payments on as an afterthought. Stellar was designed from day one for one thing: moving any asset, anywhere, for almost nothing. CrossTrade sits directly on top of that rails-first architecture.

01 / PATH PAYMENTS

Crypto ↔ fiat in a single hop

Send USDC, deliver Nigerian naira. Send euros, receive XLM. Stellar's path payment engine finds the cheapest route through the order books — the conversion happens inside one atomic transaction, never two.

02 / FINALITY

~5 second settlement, forever

No mempool, no miners, no reorgs. Stellar Consensus Protocol closes a ledger every five seconds with absolute finality — what you see confirmed is irreversible.

03 / COST

Fees measured in fractions of a cent

A swap costs 0.00001 XLM in network fees — roughly a hundred-thousandth of a dollar. Cross-border no longer means cross-purpose pricing.

04 / ANCHORS

Regulated fiat on & off-ramps

Licensed anchors issue fiat-backed tokens 1:1 and redeem them straight to your bank account. On-ramp with a card, off-ramp to an IBAN — all without leaving the app.

05 / SELF-CUSTODY

Your keys, your assets

CrossTrade never holds your funds. Trades sign locally in your wallet — Freighter, LOBSTR, or xBull — and settle straight to your own Stellar address.

How it works

Three steps to
your first swap

01

Connect a wallet

Freighter, LOBSTR, or xBull — any Stellar wallet works. No email, no sign-up, no custody. Your address is your account.

02

Pick any two assets

XLM to USDC, dollars to Bitcoin, euros to pesos. CrossTrade routes through the Stellar DEX to find you the best path and price.
